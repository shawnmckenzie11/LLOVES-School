# MCF3M B1.1

Official Ministry sample problems / e.g. examples only.

calculator, paper and pencil, graphing technology

patterning; finding values from a graph; interpreting the exponent laws

The exponent laws suggest that 4^{1/2} × 4^{1/2} = 4^1. What value would you assign to 4^{1/2}? What value would you assign to 27^{1/3}? Explain your reasoning. Extend your reasoning to make a generalization about the meaning of x^{1/n}, where x > 0 and n is a natural number.
