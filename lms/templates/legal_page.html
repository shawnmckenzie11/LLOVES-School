Request access

Teachers, families, and school administrators who are not on ALC yet can ask to join the pilot.

  1. Open the school home.
  2. Choose Request access.
  3. Say whether you are a teacher, a family, or a school administrator, plus the grade band, the subject, and how you would use ALC.
  4. Submit the form. ALC writes back at the address on the form when an invite is ready. Submitting does not create an account.
