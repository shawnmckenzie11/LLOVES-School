# MCT4C B3.6

Official Ministry sample problems / e.g. examples only.

The formula s = ut + at relates the distance, s, travelled by an object to its initial velocity, u, acceleration, a, and the elapsed time, t. Determine the acceleration of a dragster that travels 500 m from rest in 15 s, by first isolating a, and then by first substituting known values. Compare and evaluate the two methods.
