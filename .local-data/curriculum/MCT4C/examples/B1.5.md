# MCT4C B1.5

Official Ministry sample problems / e.g. examples only.

A box with no top is being made out of a 20-cm by 30-cm piece of cardboard by cutting equal squares of side length x from the corners and folding up the sides. The volume of the box is V = x(20 - 2x)(30 - 2x). Determine the volume if the side length of each square is 6 cm. Use the graph of the polynomial function V(x) to determine the size of square that should be cut from the corners if the required volume of the box is 1000 cm .
