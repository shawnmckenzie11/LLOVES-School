# MDM4U D1.4

Official Ministry sample problems / e.g. examples only.

the meaning of a statistic that is accurate to within 3 percentage points, 19 times out of 20

dynamic statistical software

larger sample sizes create higher confidence levels for a given margin of error

Use census data from Statistics Canada to investigate, using dynamic statistical software, the minimum sample size such that the proportion of the sample opting for a particular consumer or voting choice is within 3 percentage points of the proportion of the population, 95% of the time (i.e., 19 times out of 20).
