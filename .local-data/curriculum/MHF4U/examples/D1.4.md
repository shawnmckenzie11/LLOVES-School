Fluorine-20 is a radioactive substance that decays over time. At time 0, the mass of a sample of the substance is 20 g. The mass decreases to 10 g after 11 s, to 5 g after 22 s, and to 2.5 g after 33 s. Compare the average rate of change over the 33-s interval with the average rate of change over consecutive 11-s intervals.
