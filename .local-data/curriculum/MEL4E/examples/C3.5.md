# MEL4E C3.5

Official Ministry sample problems / e.g. examples only.

applying fertilizers; mixing gasoline and oil for use in small engines; mixing cement; buying plants for flower beds; using pool or laundry chemicals; doubling recipes; estimating cooking time from the time needed per pound; determining the fibre content of different sizes of food servings

Bring the label from a large can of stew to class. Use the information on the label to calculate how many calories and how much fat you would consume if you ate the whole can for dinner. Then search out information on a form of exercise you could choose for burning all those calories. For what length of time would you need to exercise?
