# MCF3M C3.3

Official Ministry sample problems / e.g. examples only.

The height above the ground of a rider on a Ferris wheel can be modelled by the sine function h(x) = 25 sin(x − 90°) + 27, where h(x) is the height, in metres, and x is the angle, in degrees, that the radius from the centre of the ferris wheel to the rider makes with the horizontal. Graph the function, using graphing technology in degree mode, and determine the maximum and minimum heights of the rider and the measures of the angle when the height of the rider is 40 m.
