# MCT4C B3.3

Official Ministry sample problems / e.g. examples only.

a constant can refer to a known initial value or a known fixed rate; a variable changes with varying conditions

The formula P = P + kh is used to determine the pressure, P kilopascals, at a depth of h metres under water, where k kilopascals per metre is the rate of change of the pressure as the depth increases, and P kilopascals is the pressure at the surface. Identify and describe the roles of P, P , k, and h in this relationship, and explain your reasoning.
