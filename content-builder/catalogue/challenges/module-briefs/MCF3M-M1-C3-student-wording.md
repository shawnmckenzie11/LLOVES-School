## Team challenge (student-facing)

A fountain’s water path in a courtyard is modelled by
\[
y = -0.2(x - 3)^2 + 4.5
\]
(with \(x\) in metres along the courtyard). The courtyard runs from \(x = 0\) to \(x = 8\). Water cannot go underground (\(y \ge 0\)).

**Graph or table first** (do not start from a formula):

1. What heights are possible for water on this path?
2. Which \(x\)-values make sense in this courtyard?

Defend your domain and range from the graph or table.
