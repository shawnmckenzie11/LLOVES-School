The rate at which a car consumes gasoline depends on the speed of the car. Use a given graph of gasoline consumption, in litres per 100 km, versus speed, in kilometres per hour, to determine how much gasoline is used to drive 500 km at speeds of 80 km/h, 100 km/h, and 120 km/h. Use the current price of gasoline to calculate the cost of driving 500 km at each of these speeds.
