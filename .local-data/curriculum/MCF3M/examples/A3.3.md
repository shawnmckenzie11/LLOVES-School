Official Ministry sample problems / e.g. examples only.

given the equation of a quadratic function representing the height of a ball over elapsed time, answer questions that involve the maximum height of the ball, the length of time needed for the ball to touch the ground, and the time interval when the ball is higher than a given measurement

In a DC electrical circuit, the relationship between the power used by a device, P (in watts, W), the electric potential difference (voltage), V (in volts, V), the current, I (in amperes, A), and the resistance, R (in ohms, Ω), is represented by the formula P = IV − I^2 R. Represent graphically and algebraically the relationship between the power and the current when the electric potential difference is 24 V and the resistance is 1.5 Ω. Determine the current needed in order for the device to use the maximum amount of power.
