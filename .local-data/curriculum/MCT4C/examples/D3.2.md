# MCT4C D3.2

Official Ministry sample problems / e.g. examples only.

A circular lake has a diameter of 4 km. Points A and D are on opposite sides of the lake and lie on a straight line through the centre of the lake, with each point 5 km from the centre. In the route ABCD, AB and CD are tangents to the lake and BC is an arc along the shore of the lake. How long is this route?
