# MBF3C A1.9

Official Ministry sample problems / e.g. examples only.

break-even point

On planet X, the height, h metres, of an object fired upward from the ground at 48 m/s is described by the equation h = 48t - 16t , where t seconds is the time since the object was fired upward. Determine the maximum height of the object, the times at which the object is 32 m above the ground, and the time at which the object hits the ground.
