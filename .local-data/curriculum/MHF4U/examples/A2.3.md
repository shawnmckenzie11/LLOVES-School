# MHF4U A2.3

Official Ministry sample problems / e.g. examples only.

Investigate the graphs of f(x) = log (x) + c, f(x) = log (x - d), various values of c, d, a, and k, using technology, describe the effects of changing these parameters in terms of transformations, and make connections to the transformations of other functions such as polynomial functions, exponential functions, and trigonometric functions.
