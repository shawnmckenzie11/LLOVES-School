# MCF3M A1.5

Official Ministry sample problems / e.g. examples only.

The profit, P, of a video company, in thousands of dollars, is given by P = −5x^2 + 550x − 5000, where x is the amount spent on advertising, in thousands of dollars. Determine, by factoring and by graphing, the amount spent on advertising that will result in a profit of $0. Describe the connection between the two strategies.
