A) \(a < 0\)  
B) \(a > 0\)  
C) \(a = 0\)  
D) Can’t tell from this picture  

**C1-CONS-2**

A) \(c > 0\)  
B) \(c < 0\)  
C) \(c = 0\)  
D) \(c\) could be anything  

**C1-CONS-3**

A) \(b > 0\)  
B) \(b < 0\)  
C) \(b = 0\)  
D) \(b\) is free — we’d need more info  

**C1-CONS-4**

**C1-CONS-5**
