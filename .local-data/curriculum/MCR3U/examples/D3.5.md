# MCR3U D3.5

Official Ministry sample problems / e.g. examples only.

The height above the ground of a rider on a Ferris wheel can be modelled by the sinusoidal function h(t) = 25sin(3(t – 30)) + 27, where h(t) is the height, in metres, and t is the time, in seconds. Graph the function, using graphing technology in degree mode, and determine the maximum and minimum heights of the rider, the height after 30 s, and the time required to complete one revolution.
